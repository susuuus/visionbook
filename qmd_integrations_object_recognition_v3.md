What is a chair? The definition is based on affordances (i.e., something you can sit on) and on social conventions (i.e., some surfaces you can sit on are meant to be used to support other objects than yourself). Affordances are likely to be directly accessible through vision, while social conventions might not be.

What is this? This drawing from Biederman @Biederman1987, shows a novel object. Example of a precision-recall curve. This is a standard plot to evaluate detection algorithms. It is usually summarized by the area under the curve.
